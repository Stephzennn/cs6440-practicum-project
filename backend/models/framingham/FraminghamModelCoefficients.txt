FRAMINGHAM OFFSPRING DIABETES RISK MODELS (2007)
Source: Wilson PWF et al. Prediction of incident diabetes mellitus in
middle-aged adults: the Framingham Offspring Study.
Arch Intern Med. 2007;167(10):1068-1074. doi:10.1001/archinte.167.10.1068

Outcome: incident type 2 diabetes over ~7-8 years (paper uses both terms)

Model type: logistic regression

Values below are odds ratios (OR). Coefficient (beta) = ln(OR).

Intercept is already on the log-odds scale.

Risk = 1 / (1 + exp(-(intercept + sum of beta for each factor present)))

VARIABLE DEFINITIONS
- Age groups: <50 (reference), 50-64, >=65
- Male: yes/no (female = reference)
- Parental history: diabetes in one or both natural parents
- BMI groups: <25.0 (reference), 25.0-29.9, >=30.0
- Blood pressure: >=130/85 mmHg or receiving treatment
  (table header says >130/85; methods section says >=130/85)
- Low HDL-C: <40 mg/dL in men, <50 mg/dL in women
- High triglycerides: >=150 mg/dL
- Large waist: >102 cm in men, >88 cm in women
- Impaired fasting glucose: 100-126 mg/dL
- Units: HDL mg/dL x 0.0259 = mmol/L; glucose mg/dL x 0.0555 = mmol/L;
  triglycerides mg/dL x 0.0113 = mmol/L



NOTE NOTE NOTE: Values are odds ratios (OR) as published, not regression coefficients.
Convert each to a beta with the natural log: beta = ln(OR).
The intercept is already a beta (log-odds scale); use it as-is.
